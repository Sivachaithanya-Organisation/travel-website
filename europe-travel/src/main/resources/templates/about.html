Why Wayfarer

Built for the trip planning that actually matters.

Wayfarer is a small, opinionated guide to twelve European cities. No listicles, no affiliate-driven "top 47 things to do" — just the season to go, roughly what a day costs, and two or three things worth rearranging your itinerary for.

Every destination page is short on purpose. If you want the full history of a city, there are better books for that. This is the version you read on the train, twenty minutes before you arrive.

This site runs on Spring Boot and Thymeleaf, and ships as a single Maven-built jar — clone it, add your own cities, and it's yours.
